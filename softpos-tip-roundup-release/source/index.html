Step 1 · Bill

Open the bill as usual

Nothing changes here. Staff tap Pay full bill and the tip screen opens.

Step 2 · Tip screen

Round the total up

Under the usual tip tiles, a new row: Or round the total up to. Each chip shows the extra amount and its percentage.

Step 3 · Pick a total

One tap, neat total

Staff tap the total the guest asks for, here 40.00. The total and the Confirm button change to S$40.00.

Step 4 · Paid

Recorded as a tip

The success screen splits it out: paid S$40.00, of which S$4.45 is tip.

Step 2 · Total Field

Set the total to charge

With Total Field, the row reads Or set the total to charge. It starts at the bill amount.

Step 3 · Custom page

Type the total

Staff tap Enter amount and type the total the guest wants to pay on Custom Payable Amount. Tip amount shows the difference.
